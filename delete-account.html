SJMMJ  •  Account Deletion Request

You can request the deletion of your TADBIR account and all associated data by following the steps below or by contacting us directly.

How to Request Account Deletion

Account deletion requests must be submitted through your Kabila Agewan. Your agewan can raise a deletion request on your behalf to the admin.

Or Contact Us Directly

You can also email us directly at sjmmjnadiad@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and include your registered phone number. We will process your request within 7 days.

What Data Gets Deleted

  • Your name, phone number, city, date of birth, and profile information
  • Your event participation requests (majlish / jamanvar)
  • Your book issue and return history
  • Your kabila and city transfer request history
  • Your attendance records and summary
  • Your FCM device token and login session

What Data May Be Retained

  • Anonymous usage logs may be retained for up to 30 days for security purposes
