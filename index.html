TADBIR – Privacy Policy

SJMMJ  •  Last updated: July 2026

TADBIR is a community management application developed for SJMMJ. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Full name, phone number, city, and date of birth provided during registration
  • Gender, blood group, profession, and kabila (community group) information
  • Device token for push notifications (via Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • Device identifier used to manage login sessions across devices
  • App usage data stored in Firebase Firestore (events, requests, notices, notes)
  • Attendance records for kabila meetings marked by your agewan
  • Book issue and return history from the community library
  • Kabila and city transfer request history

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide access to community notices, events, and announcements
  • To send relevant push notifications based on your city, kabila, and role
  • To manage event participation requests (majlish / jamanvar)
  • To track kabila meeting attendance and generate attendance summaries
  • To manage library book requests, issues, and returns
  • To process kabila and city transfer requests submitted by your agewan
  • To display your profile and community information within the app
  • To verify your identity via OTP during registration and new device login
  • To manage your login session and enforce 90-day session expiry for security

3. Data Storage

All data is securely stored using Google Firebase (Firestore, Firebase Authentication, and Firebase Cloud Messaging). Firebase complies with GDPR and other international data protection standards. We do not store data on any third-party servers outside of Firebase, except for push notification delivery which uses Firebase Cloud Messaging infrastructure.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties. Your data is only accessible to authorized administrators and agewans of SJMMJ within the scope of their community role.

5. Push Notifications

TADBIR uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send push notifications about community events, notices, assignments, attendance sessions, book approvals, and updates. Notifications are targeted by city, kabila, gender, or individual user as appropriate. You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

6. Session Management

TADBIR uses a secure session system to manage your login. A unique session token is stored on your device using encrypted storage. Sessions expire after 90 days of inactivity, after which you will be required to log in again. Logging in on a new device requires OTP verification for security.

7. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. Past events, expired notices, and old notifications are periodically purged by administrators to keep the app clean. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

8. Children's Privacy

TADBIR is intended for use by community members aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including Firebase security rules, server-side role verification via Cloud Functions, encrypted session tokens, and OTP-based device verification to protect your personal information. Sensitive operations such as account creation, password changes, and banning are handled exclusively through Cloud Functions and are never accessible directly from the client.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
